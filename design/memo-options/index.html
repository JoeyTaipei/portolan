Meeting memo: where the extra questions go

Each option keeps the 30-second debrief exactly as today. They differ in where the optional detail opens: the influence answers (which feed the score), attitude and priorities, and what to expect. Every detail question has a dashed Not sure choice that records nothing. Example: a meeting with the drone industry alliance, sample data.
