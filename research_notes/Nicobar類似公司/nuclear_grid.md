# Nicobar Group benchmark and similar small market-entry consultancies in nuclear power and electric grid (as of Nov 2026)

Method note: newnicobarwebsite.squarespace.com (Nicobar's archived site) could not be fetched from this session (DNS blocked / crawl timeout), so the Nicobar facts below come from LinkedIn data, a 2018 Business Wire release, and The Lantau Group's own pages. Search snippets show that site still hosts Nicobar pages, including "certification-advisory-haf604-en", "strategy-development-and-execution" and weekly "China nuclear news roundup" posts through Dec 2019. Employee counts and revenue marked "(aggregator)" come from LinkedIn/data-vendor profiles returned by Exa search, which are **low reliability**. Companies already covered in reports/海外市場進入產品比較.md and research_notes/海外市場進入產品比較/ were checked; none of the firms below appear there.

## Q1. Is the Nicobar Group profile correct, and what happened after the Lantau Group deal?

### Takeaway
The profile is broadly right. Nicobar Group Ltd. was a Shanghai-based niche consultancy for China's civil nuclear sector, offering market intelligence, market entry, lead generation, supply chain/sourcing and HAF604 certification support. Its team (not necessarily the whole legal entity) went to The Lantau Group in 2019, first through an "alliance" and then an "acquisition of the team". Terms were never published. The Nicobar brand appears dormant since about 2020, and the former Nicobar staff now do mainly China power-market and green-power work inside TLG's Shanghai office.

Headquartered in Shanghai, Nicobar's services includes China market intelligence, market entry support, strategy development, project management, China lead generation and China supply chain and sourcing for the nuclear industry." ### Inferences
- Corrections to the benchmark description:
  - "Acquired" should read "**team acquired (acqui-hire) by The Lantau Group in 2019 after an alliance**".
  - The Lantau Group is a ~50–70 person HK/Singapore APAC energy-economics consultancy (a CRA International HK spin-out MBO), not a nuclear firm.
  - After the deal, the nuclear market-entry service (HAF604, lead generation) seems to have been absorbed into general China power advisory. I found no evidence that TLG markets HAF604 services today.
- "North American vendors" is consistent with the evidence: the US engineering-consulting client, the Toronto partner, and the Canadian/US-educated team. However, the client list is not public.
- The Nicobar brand is effectively inactive. Its domain appears lost, and its LinkedIn has been silent since 2020.

### Gaps
- Nicobar's exact founding date, legal entity (WFOE vs HK company), founders, headcount by year, revenue, client names, fee structure, and deal value/terms: no primary source found. The Wayback/archived site was not reachable in this session.
- Exact date of the TLG Shanghai office opening (page undated).

## Q2. Which similar small market-entry consultancies exist in nuclear and grid, and how closely do they match?

### Takeaway
The closest analogues are DB2 Consulting with its Chinese counterpart Dynabond Powertech/Dynatom (nuclear, Canada↔China, HAF604, plus Korea and Argentina representatives), EMCC Korea/EMCC Global (nuclear, Korea→US, NQA-1 and vendor registration, with a **published fixed-program + success-fee model**), and Nicobar's former partner Strategic Insights (now apparently inactive). In the grid sector, the market is served by tiny one-to-five-person representative/homologation boutiques: TIPMC (Austria), EU-Power (Netherlands), Power Services & Consulting (UAE), and NAMERA (Turkey). Certification-only shops (Cisema, MPR) cover HAF604 but are multi-industry.

Criteria key: (a) small (≤~50 staff), single-industry; (b) gated government/SOE market; (c) market intelligence / leads / certification / local execution; (d) two countries or a partner on the other side.

### Cited Findings

**1. DB2 Consulting Inc.** — nuclear — **(a)(b)(c)(d) ✔✔✔✔**
- HQ Cobourg/Baltimore, Ontario, Canada; founded 2015; 2 employees (aggregator); ISO 9001:2015; member of OCI and the Canadian Nuclear Isotope Council — [LinkedIn](https://linkedin.com/company/db2-consulting-inc)
- Services are "exclusively focused in the nuclear industry": business development, market analysis, technical consulting. CANDU, PWR, SMR. A "Chinese Chief Representative based in Beijing" handles "market analyses, business development services and HAF 604 Certification"; Chief Representatives in Seoul and Buenos Aires cover Korea and Argentina. Claims: "over $125 million in sales for our clients" and representation of clients in over 6 countries (self-reported) — [db2consulting.ca](https://db2consulting.ca/)
- Its Chinese counterpart is Dynabond Powertech Service (Jan 2025 post). In May 2025 it posted HAF604 outreach — [LinkedIn](https://linkedin.com/company/db2-consulting-inc)
- Fee model: not public. Status: independent, active.

**2. Dynabond Powertech Service (北京代邦能源技术服务有限公司) / Dynatom International GmbH** — nuclear — **(a)? (b)(c)(d) ✔**

**4. EMCC Korea / EMCC Global LLC** — nuclear — **(a)? (b)(c)(d) ✔**

### Inferences
- The Nicobar model has two halves: a vendor-side partner abroad, and an in-country team doing intelligence, leads, certification and on-the-ground follow-up. Today that model survives mainly as **micro-firms (1–5 people) with a "chief representative" in the target country** (DB2 + Dynabond; EMCC Korea + EMCC Global). Larger firms that once did it (Nicobar → TLG) moved up-market into power-economics advisory.
- Public pricing is rare. The only explicit model found is EMCC's fixed 5-month program plus a success fee on net export sales, which is a useful benchmark for Portolan's fixed-price dossier versus success-fee options.
- Grid-sector boutiques are almost all "manufacturer's representative + homologation" shops for European TSOs/GCC utilities. I found **no small independent firm specifically for State Grid China, KEPCO, Taipower or PGCIL type testing**. Those gates seem to be handled by large certifiers (TÜV), local agents/distributors, or the vendors' own BD staff.
- Several self-reported metrics (DB2's "$125M in client sales", Cisema's "nearly 100 specialists", MPR's "300 factories") are unverified marketing claims.

### Gaps
- Founding years, headcounts and fees for EMCC, Dynabond, TIPMC, EU-Power, PSC and Strategic Insights: not public.
- No small independent market-entry firm found for India NPCIL, UAE ENEC or Eastern Europe (Poland, Czechia) nuclear, beyond EMCC's stated emerging-market consulting and KHNP-KNP's Warsaw plan. More LinkedIn searching would be needed.
- No small specialist found for China State Grid/CSG supplier qualification, KEPCO grid vendor registration, or Taipower. This may be a real market gap or a search limitation (Chinese/Korean-language sources were not searched in depth).
- Strategic Insights' formal closure date: not found.
